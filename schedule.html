How Power Rankings Work: Each team starts at 1500. After each game, ratings adjust by:
NewR = R + 20 × (Actual − Expected), where Expected = 1/(1+10^{(Opp−R)/400}).

Expected W-L: Pythagorean expectation W% = RS²/(RS²+RA²) × games played.
